# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained as a professional engineering workspace. Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, specific, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Critique code, designs, and assumptions rather than people.
- Keep security-sensitive details private until they are fixed or safely disclosed.
- Respect maintainers' time by including reproduction steps, context, and test results.
- Use inclusive language and avoid harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information, credentials, customer data, or vulnerability details without consent.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or intentionally derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject contributions; or block participants when behavior undermines a safe and productive project environment.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately with the relevant context and links.
